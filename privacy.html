On Second Tap Privacy Policy

On Second Tap uses Apple's Screen Time tools to apply the rules you choose on your iPhone. The app and its Screen Time extensions share your selections, website list, schedules, settings and unlock history locally.

If you choose a walking or shaking challenge, the app uses step counts or motion readings on your device to measure progress during the challenge.

On Second Tap has no accounts, advertising or analytics SDKs. The app does not transmit your selections, motion readings or unlock history to the developer or advertisers.

You can edit or remove rules and websites in the app and revoke Screen Time access in iPhone Settings. If you contact support, the information you choose to send is used to respond to your request.
